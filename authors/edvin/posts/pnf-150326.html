CRAR stretching

Topic: Motion | Subtopic: Flexibility | Date: 2026 03/13

Contract-Relax (CR): Enter the passive stretch and then Contract the muscle you want to stretch. In the stretched position it adds stability to the joint -> your brain is willing to let you go further. Be careful, have some self-control.

Antagonist-Contract (AR): Contract the antagonist muscle (the opposite muscle you want to stretch). Due to reciprocal inhibition, the neural mechanism where activation of a muscle automatically reduces activation of its antagonist, you'll get deeper into the stretch.

CRAC combines the two:

  1. Stretch target muscle
  2. CR
  3. relax
  4. AR
